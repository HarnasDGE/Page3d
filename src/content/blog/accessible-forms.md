The basics that make forms usable for everyone.

## Real labels

Every input needs a visible label tied to it. Placeholders are not labels.

## Helpful errors

Show errors next to the field, in text, and announce them to screen readers.

## Keyboard first

Tab through the whole form. If you get stuck or lost, so will your users.
