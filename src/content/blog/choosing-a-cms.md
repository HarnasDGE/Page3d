Matching the content tool to the team that will use it.

## Who edits, how often

A weekly blog needs a different tool than a site updated twice a year.

## Headless or not

Headless CMSs give flexibility and speed; all-in-one tools are simpler for small teams.

## Test with the editors

Let the people who will use it try two options before deciding.
