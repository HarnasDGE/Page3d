How I price projects without undercharging or scaring clients.

## Price the outcome

Estimate the effort, then consider the value the project creates for the client.

## Milestones

Split payment into milestones tied to deliverables, not dates.

## A clear change process

New ideas are welcome, and they come with a small written estimate.
