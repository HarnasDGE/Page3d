Using SVG filters to generate asphalt, grime and noise for 3D scenes.

## feTurbulence is noise

The turbulence filter gives you Perlin-style noise with a seed, and `stitchTiles` makes it seamless.

## Tiny files

A whole asphalt texture is under 2 kB of SVG, rasterised by the browser once.

## Easy to tweak

Colour matrices let you tune contrast and tint without touching an image editor.
