Short, clear proposals win more work than long ones.

## Lead with their problem

Open with what you heard in the discovery call, in their words.

## One page of scope

Deliverables, timeline and price should fit on a single page.

## Make yes easy

End with the next step and a date, not a vague offer to chat.
