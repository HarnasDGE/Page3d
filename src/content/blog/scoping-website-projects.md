Questions I ask before quoting a new site.

## Goals before pages

What should the site achieve in six months? Leads, sales, hiring?

## Content reality check

Who writes the copy, and when? Missing content is the most common cause of delays.

## Define done

Agree on browsers, devices, languages and what is out of scope.
