UX lessons from building a site you walk through.

## Always show the way

Signposts, street names and a visible exit keep people oriented.

## Two ways to move

Keyboard and joystick for gamers, tap-to-walk for everyone else.

## Content still wins

Long text belongs in a readable HTML panel, not on a tiny 3D screen.
