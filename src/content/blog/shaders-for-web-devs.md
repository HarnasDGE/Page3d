A gentle introduction to GLSL for people who write JavaScript.

## Think per pixel

A fragment shader runs once for every pixel, in parallel. You describe the colour of one pixel.

## Start from UVs

Map UV coordinates to colour first, then add time, noise and distance fields.

## Borrow, then learn

Read The Book of Shaders and remix examples before writing from scratch.
