What changed in v4 and how it affects real projects.

## CSS-first configuration

Design tokens live in `@theme` inside your stylesheet. No JavaScript config needed for most projects.

## Faster builds

The new engine rebuilds in milliseconds, which you notice on large sites.

## Modern CSS by default

Container queries, `@starting-style` and wide-gamut colours are available out of the box.
